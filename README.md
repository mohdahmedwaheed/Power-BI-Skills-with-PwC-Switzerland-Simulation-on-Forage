# Unlocking Insights: Power BI Virtual Case Experience with PwC

Welcome to the Unlocking Insights: Power BI Virtual Case Experience with PwC! This engaging program is designed to equip you with the essential skills needed to create impactful dashboards using Power BI. In this experience, you'll tackle real-world challenges that businesses face, focusing on customer retention, churn analysis, and diversity & inclusion metrics.

## Task 2 - Customer Retention
### Objective
Create a dynamic dashboard in Power BI that showcases all relevant Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and metrics from the provided dataset. This dashboard will help visualize key aspects of customer retention.
### Possible KPIs to Include
### Overall Customer Satisfaction: 
Measure how satisfied customers are with your services.
### Overall Calls Answered/Abandoned: 
Track the efficiency of your customer service team.
### Calls by Time: 
Analyze call volume trends over different times of the day or week.
### Average Speed of Answer: 
Evaluate how quickly customer calls are being answered.
### Agent's Performance Quadrant: 
Compare average handle time (talk duration) against the number of calls answered to assess agent performance.

## Task 3 - Customer Churn
### Objective
Develop a comprehensive dashboard that reflects customer churn metrics. This analysis will provide insights into why customers leave and how to improve retention strategies.
### Inputs to Consider
### Customers Who Left Within the Last Month: 
Identify recent churners for targeted outreach.
### Services Each Customer Has Signed Up For: 
Analyze which services are associated with higher churn rates (e.g., phone, internet, streaming).
### Customer Account Information: 
Include details such as:
### Duration as a customer
### Contract type
### Payment method
### Paperless billing status
### Monthly charges and total charges
### Number of tickets opened (administrative and technical)
### Demographic Info: 
Gather insights on gender, age range, partners, and dependents.

## Task 4 - Diversity & Inclusion
### Objective
Create a dashboard that defines relevant KPIs related to hiring, promotion, performance, and turnover. This analysis will help draw conclusions about diversity and inclusion within the organization.
### Key Measures to Create
### % of Men/Women: 
Understand gender representation in the workforce.
### % of Leavers: 
Track employee turnover rates.
### % Employees Promoted (FY21): 
Evaluate promotion rates across demographics.
### % of Women Promoted: 
Assess gender equity in promotions.
### % of Hires by Gender: 
Analyze hiring practices for gender balance.
### Average Performance Rating: 
Compare performance ratings between men and women.
